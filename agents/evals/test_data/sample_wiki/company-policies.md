# Company Policies

HR policies and guidelines for Acme Analytics employees.

## Time Off

### PTO Policy

All full-time employees receive:
- **Vacation:** 20 days per year (accrues monthly)
- **Sick leave:** 10 days per year
- **Personal days:** 3 days per year

**Carryover:** Up to 5 vacation days may carry over to the next year.

### Requesting Time Off

1. Submit request in BambooHR at least 2 weeks in advance
2. Manager approval required
3. For >5 consecutive days, VP approval also required
4. Notify your team in Slack #team-calendar

### Holidays

Acme Analytics observes the following holidays (2026):
- New Year's Day — January 1
- MLK Day — January 20
- Presidents Day — February 17
- Memorial Day — May 26
- Independence Day — July 4
- Labor Day — September 1
- Thanksgiving — November 27-28
- Winter Break — December 24-26, December 31

## Remote Work

### Hybrid Policy

Engineering team operates on a flexible hybrid schedule:
- **Required in-office:** Tuesday, Wednesday
- **Optional in-office:** Monday, Thursday, Friday
- **Fully remote:** Available by manager approval

### Work Hours

Core hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM PT
- All team members should be available during core hours
- Meetings should be scheduled during core hours when possible
- Async communication preferred outside core hours

### Home Office Setup

Company provides:
- Laptop (MacBook Pro 14" or equivalent)
- External monitor (up to $500)
- Keyboard and mouse
- Desk and chair (up to $800)

Submit equipment requests through IT Help Desk.

## Security Policies

### Access Control

- All systems require SSO via Okta
- 2FA required for production access
- Laptop encryption mandatory (FileVault)
- Password manager required (1Password)

### Data Handling

- Customer data must not be stored locally
- PII requires encryption at rest and in transit
- No customer data in Slack, email, or docs
- Use anonymized data for testing

### Incident Response

If you suspect a security incident:
1. Do not attempt to investigate alone
2. Contact security@acme-analytics.com immediately
3. Preserve any evidence (don't delete/modify)
4. Document timeline of events

## Performance Reviews

### Review Cycle

- **Quarterly check-ins:** Manager 1:1 with written feedback
- **Annual review:** Comprehensive performance evaluation (January)
- **Promotion cycles:** Twice yearly (April, October)

### Performance Ratings

| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Exceeds | Consistently surpasses expectations |
| Meets | Fully delivers on role requirements |
| Developing | Growing toward full competency |
| Needs Improvement | Performance concerns to address |

### Feedback Process

1. Self-assessment (employee)
2. Manager assessment
3. Peer feedback (optional, 2-3 peers)
4. Calibration meeting (leadership)
5. Review conversation (manager + employee)
